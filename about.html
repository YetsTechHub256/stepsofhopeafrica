About Steps of Hope Africa

SOHA is a charity organization based in Uganda rebuilding hope through education, health and empowerment.

History

We are Steps of Hope Africa (SOHA) founded by the young, sharp, brilliant minded students from Mountains of the Moon University who are dedicated to put a difference positively in a community. It's located at Lake Saaka Kasindikwa area, Fort Portal city in Western Uganda.

This is a non-profit entity whose aim is to create and rebuild hope with our partners on different aspects in our community and to be a consolation centre of hope to the devastated groups of people.

The motive that inspired us to start up this foundation is to extend priceless and sincere support to people in need with the help of our knowledge from schooling, support from our partners and God helping us.
