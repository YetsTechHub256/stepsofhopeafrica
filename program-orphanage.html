Orphanage Support

Providing shelter, care, and basic needs for orphaned children in our community.

Support this program
Program Overview

Our Orphanage Support program provides a safe haven for orphaned children in our community. We offer shelter, basic needs, and a nurturing environment where these children can grow, learn, and thrive despite their circumstances.

Through the little resources we can afford, we ensure that every child in our care receives:

  • Safe Shelter: A secure and comfortable living environment
  • Basic Needs: Food, clothing, and essential supplies
  • Education Support: Access to schooling and learning materials
  • Healthcare: Medical attention and hygiene support
  • Skills Development: Life skills and vocational training
Recent Achievement

We recently purchased a sewing machine for the orphanage to help children learn practical skills and create income-generating opportunities. This equipment enables them to develop sewing and tailoring skills that can support their future independence.
