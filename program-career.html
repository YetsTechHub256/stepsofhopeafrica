Career Development

Talent, skills and entrepreneurship support for youth.

Program overview

SOHA runs career development programs to streamline back lost hope through talent development and hands-on skills training.

We encourage participation in sports, music, fashion and design, plus entrepreneurship support and business advisory services to help youth stay engaged and build livelihoods.

With combined effort, we can rebuild hope together. Your support counts.
