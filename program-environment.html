Environmental Conservation

Afforestation, clean-ups, sensitisation and domestic tourism promotion.

Support this program
Program overview

SOHA runs conservation activities including afforestation, garbage collection, sensitisation and self awareness, plus promotion of domestic tourism to uplift tourism in Western Uganda.

  • Afforestation: tree planting and anti-deforestation sensitisation
  • Garbage collection: collection of plastics, polythene bags and other waste
  • Sensitisation: awareness on the dangers of destroying the environment
  • Domestic tourism: promoting tourism sites and engaging stakeholders

With your support, we can scale these efforts and conserve our environment for future generations.

  • Seedlings & tools
  • Clean-up equipment
  • Community sensitisation materials
